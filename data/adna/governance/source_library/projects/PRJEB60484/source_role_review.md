# PRJEB60484 reindeer source roles

The [primary Molecular Ecology paper](https://doi.org/10.1111/mec.17274) states that its sequence data are under `PRJEB60484` and reports 18 ancient Svalbard reindeer in Table 1. Its published PDF is retained from the [Norwegian National Research Archive record](https://nva.sikt.no/registration/0198cc66e16f-a743d6e1-054b-400e-a2c4-dd186a645095), under CC BY 4.0, with SHA-256 `1446b3d42f066605e162cd043ae4eb2da1ef14748534af7b1205cada19d0fb3f`. The published Table 1 on page 4 confirms the preprint's `R20` interval of 0–450 BP and gives UTM easting and northing without a zone. The article points to sample metadata in supplementary Table S3. The [publisher-listed Appendix S1](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/action/downloadSupplement?doi=10.1111%2Fmec.17274&file=mec17274-sup-0001-Appendix+S1.zip) remains uncaptured because the publisher download returns HTTP 403.

The later [Scientific Reports paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-54296-2) reuses 16 samples from the project for a mitogenome analysis. Two further Franz Josef Land archive aliases match its Supplementary Table S1, although that table lists their ENA BioProject as “This study” rather than `PRJEB60484`. Its role is a secondary specimen source, not the primary publication of the archive project.

The earlier file named `41598_2024_64499_MOESM1_ESM.pdf` under the reindeer DOI was a supplement titled “An ultra high-endurance memristor using back-end-of-line amorphous SiC.” It came from a DOI-keyed local stash and was removed from the governed reindeer library. Its SHA-256 was `2d0de5cfaf1c97981cea1ca9c1c086719a5f6da9a5ea48fca1f0dae69412f1e0`; Git history preserves the prior custody mistake.

The replacement files are the publisher's actual reindeer [supplementary figures](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41598-024-54296-2/MediaObjects/41598_2024_54296_MOESM1_ESM.pdf) (SHA-256 `93889718035d8c8aface421858067ae7e151a71fa893a7df2e63cb2b10111286`) and [supplementary tables](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41598-024-54296-2/MediaObjects/41598_2024_54296_MOESM2_ESM.xlsx) (SHA-256 `1a11850c94d8a08f91a11feecc298dc9be9003911bd6dc39df6f6796c2348988`). Official ENA XML and capture receipts now cover all 20 archive BioSamples. Neither the primary paper's broad Svalbard geography nor its sample count authorizes a coordinate or date for an individual without an exact source join.

Exact ENA alias joins recover 17 localities and 16 sample-owned calibrated BP intervals from the later table. `B20` remains unjoined to its `B20a*` label; `R29a` and `R36` have no row there. The later table reports `R20` at 41–252 BP, while the published primary article and preprint both report 0–450 BP. Its locality is retained, but its date is held until the differing intervals are reconciled or a source-specific chronology selection is justified. The two Franz Josef Land specimens remain Russian evidence, outside the four-country Nordic result.

An [author bioRxiv preprint](https://doi.org/10.1101/2023.09.21.558762) (version posted September 22, 2023; SHA-256 `59862eade5dee058b4e0050001c189ec1c7edc18dea06cf15dadba353600f2af`) is retained as a distinct source role. Its Table 1 on printed pages 41–43 names all 18 Svalbard specimens and the archive project. Exact ENA alias joins add the `B20` 2329–2460 BP, `R29a` 12–267 BP, and `R36` 1–282 BP intervals. The preprint and later table agree on the other 14 admitted Svalbard intervals; they disagree for `R20` (0–450 BP in both preprint and final primary article). The preprint's `Wijdfjorden` spelling for `R29a` and `R36` is normalized to the later table's `Wijdefjorden`, supported by the same or adjacent R25a grid values; the source spelling remains in each evidence excerpt. The primary article and preprint report UTM easting and northing for all 18, but neither gives a UTM zone. Those grid values remain cited in the source and code; no WGS84 points are inferred from them. The current archive population has 19 admitted intervals and one unresolved date, with 19 locality rows but zero point coordinates.
