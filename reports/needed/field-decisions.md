## Questions and responsible resolvers

### Identity

Identify the exact physical lake/core or archaeological study parcel, native identifiers, source version and coordinates; distinguish modern registry, deposited pollen sequence and archaeological site.

**Responsible resolver:** Source custodian and qualified lake/core or archaeological identity reviewer. **Reopen when:** A dated, source-bound identity packet names the intended physical study object and resolves aliases without treating geographic proximity as core identity.

### Basin and sediment

Provide dated bathymetry or parcel stratigraphy, sediment/archive condition and the exact proposed sampling point/footprint; identify which observations support a coring or archaeological sampling design.

**Responsible resolver:** Qualified field investigator and bathymetry/sediment or archaeological archive custodian. **Reopen when:** A dated measured packet identifies the archive and proposed footprint; a qualified field reviewer records support and remaining uncertainty for that design.

### Access

Document the proposed approach, launch/entry location, transport and access conditions, evidence date and who can authorize that access.

**Responsible resolver:** Local access custodian and field logistics lead. **Reopen when:** A dated site-specific access record identifies the route, current conditions, responsible authority and any unresolved restrictions.

### Landowner

Identify the current land/water or collection custodian for the actual footprint and provide the scope, date and evidence of any permission required for the proposed activity.

**Responsible resolver:** Land/water or collection custodian and responsible project investigator. **Reopen when:** The actual rights-holder and footprint are established and a dated permission decision covers the named activity; permission is not inferred from public map availability.

### Permits

Determine which permits, notifications or other approvals apply to the exact proposed activity and location, who decides them, and the status and conditions of each.

**Responsible resolver:** Competent permitting authority and responsible project investigator. **Reopen when:** The competent authority provides a dated applicability/decision record tied to the footprint and activity; unresolved approvals remain explicit.

### Conservation and heritage

Determine the current conservation, protected-area and cultural-heritage constraints relevant to the exact footprint and activity, using authoritative dated records and a qualified interpretation.

**Responsible resolver:** Competent conservation and cultural-heritage authorities. **Reopen when:** Authoritative current constraints and their relevance to the proposed activity are recorded, including remaining restrictions and decisions; absence is not inferred from an unchecked map.

### Hazards

Provide a site-specific field risk assessment for the proposed date, transport and sampling method, identifying water/ground/weather hazards, emergency arrangements and the accountable field lead.

**Responsible resolver:** Qualified field safety lead and relevant local site custodian. **Reopen when:** A dated activity-specific assessment and operating decision identifies controls, emergency arrangements and remaining hazards before fieldwork is scheduled.

### Field decision

Return a qualified advance, defer or reject decision for the exact source-bound field proposal, with rationale, conditions, scientific objective and permissions/safety dependencies.

**Responsible resolver:** Responsible scientific field lead with the relevant domain and permission reviewers. **Reopen when:** A dated signed or otherwise verifiable decision addresses this exact footprint and activity, names the evidence inspected and preserves every unmet condition; it does not imply ecological validation.
