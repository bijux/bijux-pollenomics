# Questions for independent scientific review

The [review matrix](scientific-review.csv) identifies **36 bounded decisions** that would need a qualified source owner, an independent scientific reviewer, or both before a stronger claim is made. It is a request and reading aid, not a record of completed reviews. Every row has `reviewer_assignment=unassigned`, `review_decision=not_received`, and no decision date or decision evidence. The related [source requests](source-requests.csv) are also unsent. The matrix covers all 18 currently registered request IDs; a row with `source_request_ids=none` has no matching packet in that register.

Start with the `claim_report_paths` and their parallel `claim_report_sha256` values. Read each report's supported result and explicit limit before reading the review question. The `supporting_material_paths` and parallel hashes identify the exact retained receipt, source account or presentation claim that framed the question. Each semicolon-separated path aligns by position with its semicolon-separated SHA-256. A changed byte invalidates that row's version binding and calls for a refreshed question, even if the file name is unchanged. The matrix also pins the [61-account claim register](report-claims.csv) at SHA-256 `12961007f5756d59da42faf3e133faf5bda2ab7c04598fa0210f47a99e05aa5d` and the unsent source-request register at SHA-256 `aec2f10a8beaf9f2363d44a254346035cf61ed875ad9834a8a49a15543e57fb3`.

Read the `precise_review_question` with `decision_scope`: one decision cannot approve adjacent populations, methods or uses. The LandClim author example checks a later two-grid calculation; it does not identify all 1,128 historical inputs. A core chronology decision does not approve its basin or count denominator. A finite LOVE calculation may still yield invalid vegetation composition. A direct-pollen score concerns its declared 16-, 30- or 31-label subcomposition and its exposed cohort; it is not a measured-cover score. The current independent Nordic vegetation-pair denominator is zero. Those boundaries are also stated in the [scientific briefing](../../presentation/scientific-evidence.tex) and [collaborator briefing](../../presentation/big-picture.tex).

The `required_source_role` names who could authenticate original rows, methods or use terms. The `qualified_reviewer_role` and `qualification_and_independence` identify the different judgment needed to interpret those materials. A source provider's permission answer does not itself establish scientific comparability, and a numerical replay does not confer source rights. Where a source owner also wishes to review interpretation, record their relationship to the material and obtain the stated independent assessment before promoting a claim. No person has been nominated in this draft.

For an actual review, make a copy of the [decision form](review-decision.md) for **one review ID and one immutable set of materials**. Record the source packet and its terms, the reviewer's qualifications and conflicts, the exact accepted or refused claim, the rationale and evidence path, and any conditions. A dated decision belongs only in the completed record. Then rerun or revise the named `admission_consumer_paths` only for the question answered; retain rejected, missing and not-evaluated results. `unresolved_consequence` says what must remain limited until that happens, and `reopening_trigger` names the evidence that would justify revisiting the question.

The matrix was assembled from existing reports and requests. It did not acquire sources, rerun old studies, open untouched target outcomes, determine licences, or obtain an independent reader's judgment. [The verifier](verify-review.py) checks the versioned claim and supporting-material hashes, all 18 request IDs and the exact source-request register bytes, all listed material/consumer paths, and the 36 review IDs without rerunning scientific studies.
