# Why we separate research decisions from generated reports

## Our decision

We use root [`reports/`](../README.md) as the reader's route from a question
to a result, its source and its limits. A domain report owns one scientific
interpretation. The [claim index](../evidence-index.md) links that
interpretation to the governed numeric rows and maintained producer. The
[`reports/needed/`](../needed/README.md) register holds genuine unanswered
source questions; completed work and execution plans do not belong there.

We keep `docs/report/` as an active governed output and website source tree
while its producers still write there. We move a result only with its
producer, tests, manifest and incoming citations, so it has one numeric
owner after the move. A former website path may remain as a short citation
to that owner. Selected rights-safe explanations appear under `docs/public/`;
the [PDF briefings](../../presentation/README.md) cite locked report versions.
We treat untracked `artifacts/` as disposable. Any current consumer that still
requires an old run path violates this ownership boundary and needs migration.

This separation matters because the current `docs/report/` tree has multiple
owners. The repository publisher, source consumers, numerical studies and
website assets do not share one regeneration command. An isolated
`publish-reports` run can repeat its own outputs without reproducing every
tracked file produced by those other owners. We therefore require a
producer-specific replay and tracked-byte comparison before claiming that a
migrated result is reproducible. The [report verifier](../verify.py) checks
reader inventory, local links and selected source identities; the
[scientific verifier](../../docs/report/pollen-reconstruction/verify_report.py)
checks its governed result set. Neither alone proves a full clean-tree
rebuild. The [rebuild policy](../../configs/ci/reproducible-report-build.json)
must be satisfied against every actual producer before that broader claim.

We require independent report producers to write into fresh `artifacts/`
outputs for each of two rebuild lanes. Their owner receipts bind the command,
declared input paths and bytes, generated bytes and exact report paths; the
final comparison rejects overlap with publisher partitions and compares the
combined output to tracked `docs/report/`. This keeps the publisher's country
partitions intact while giving other producers explicit ownership. It does
not prove that a declared input list is complete, so each producer command
and its source dependencies still need review. We regenerated the three R
CSV files and native JSON in our
[Petr Kuneš comparison](../../docs/report/method-certification/petrkunes-r-comparison/README.md)
through two fresh owner lanes. Both lanes match each other and all four
tracked files byte for byte. The native calculation reads the governed R
taxa and bootstrap files, whose bytes independently match both fresh R
lanes. This proves that four-output family under its declared inputs; it
does not cover the other nonpublisher owners. We retain the full-tree
rebuild as open.

The publisher's cross-tree registry and sustainability reports depend on files
produced by those independent owners. We now pass verified owner outputs into
the reducer's staging tree, bind their paths and receipt digests in each
assembly manifest, and remove those inputs from the publisher-owned output.
The final comparison combines the separately owned files with the publisher
inventory and rejects an assembly that did not declare the inputs it consumed.
In a two-lane rebuild, the three author uncertainty outputs raised the derived
surface count from 705 to 708 and the report-file count from 709 to 712;
both publisher lanes remained byte-identical. The tracked-tree comparison
still finds 1,199 files without an owner in this bounded run and 12 derived
content differences. We therefore retain whole-tree equivalence as open
until the remaining owners are independently replayed and handed to the reducer.

We also rebuilt the [six-site author uncertainty study](../../docs/report/method-certification/author-site-uncertainty/README.md)
twice from the pinned author inputs. Its `report.json`, `draws.json` and
`cover-draws.csv` agree byte for byte across both runs and with the three
tracked outputs. Each owner receipt checks the declared source, code and
dependency files before and after the replay. This establishes those three
files as another independently reproducible report family; the study remains
a conditional ten-taxon author example, not a Nordic vegetation validation.
The [paired historical and reviewed profile comparison](../../docs/report/method-certification/author-site-profile-comparison/README.md)
then reproduced its `report.json` in two owner lanes against 59 declared
inputs, including those frozen uncertainty outputs. Both lanes match the
tracked result. The published full-grid R comparison is a separate check;
this selected ten-taxon replay did not run a new R calculation.

The separate [joint site, count and PPE draw example](../../docs/report/method-certification/joint-site-draws/README.md)
also reproduced its three numeric files in two owner lanes against 52
declared inputs. Those files demonstrate a synthetic uncertainty calculation;
they do not add a measured vegetation result.

We have bound and replayed the six [SEAD source report owners](../../docs/report/source-relations/sead/README.md#report-ownership).
They reproduce 11 governed JSON/CSV outputs byte for byte, and the six
explanatory pages have 15 resolving local links. This is a verified portion of
the mixed report tree; the remaining nonpublisher outputs still need their own
owner replay before an assembled whole-tree no-difference claim.

We also replayed the source capability, DNA scope, Rootsi–LandClim overlap,
LandClim author preparation, OpenLand intake and aggregate source
reconciliation owners. Their eight generated JSON/CSV outputs now match the
governed files. The [OpenLand decision](../../docs/report/source-relations/openland-intake/README.md)
uses the current producer identity while retaining the same 51-member ledger.
The [source reconciliation](../../docs/report/source-relations/source-reconciliation/README.md)
now records the 13 authority paths added to its verified input closure; its
accounting checks still pass without changing scientific admission. These
source-specific replays account for another part of the tree, while full
mixed-owner assembly remains open.

The five [synthetic method challenges](../../docs/report/synthetic-validation/README.md)
also replayed their five governed JSON outputs byte for byte. Their explanatory
pages are separately authored and linked from the family index. This gives
these numerical and workflow checks producer ownership without treating
fabricated cases as measured ecological validation.

We also replayed the [Neotoma first-observed readiness report](../../docs/report/propagation/source-first-observed/README.md)
in two independent owner lanes. Its 3.44 MB JSON output matched the tracked
file byte for byte from 30 declared native-source and code inputs in each lane.
The report still refuses all 256 feature and chronology attempts and admits
zero events; reproducible refusal is not evidence of an ecological route.
The [governed four-country edge ledger](../../docs/report/propagation/governed-edge-ledger/README.md)
now writes fresh CSV and JSON outputs from 15 declared inputs. Both owner
lanes match the tracked files byte for byte. Its 16 country-pair bins still
contain zero admitted events or evaluated edges, so these receipts establish
report ownership without creating an observed propagation result.

The [LandClim II study-input inventory](../../docs/report/publication-replay/landclim-study-inputs/README.md)
replayed its five generated files byte for byte from the pinned raw workbook
and receipt. Its 1,128 publication metadata rows and 4,512 explicit input
refusals are reproducible; none becomes an authenticated historical pollen
input or a publication reproduction merely because the inventory matches.

The [LandClim window alignment](../../docs/report/publication-replay/landclim-window-alignment/README.md)
now replays its three governed outputs byte for byte from the pinned author
time axis and published-window report. We fixed the CSV writer to use LF and
removed an obsolete line-ending discrepancy from its manifest. The result
still supports an exact time-bound comparison only for TW2; it does not
authenticate the other historical publication inputs.

The [buffered author-pollen comparison](../../docs/report/pollen-reconstruction/author-buffered-pollen/README.md)
has one maintained family replay for its four methods, two withheld grids and
five comparisons. We regenerated all 51 result files in two independent owner
lanes from 211 declared source, code, lock and reference inputs per lane. Both
lanes match each other and the tracked files byte for byte. This is an English
direct observed-pollen development result on previously exposed targets; it
does not establish Nordic LRA or independent vegetation skill. The family
manifest and explanatory page remain separate maintained inputs to the replay.

The [author site-mean holdout](../../docs/report/pollen-reconstruction/author-example-site-mean-holdout/README.md)
now has the same exact owner route for four generated files. Its replay checks
the private source and producer identity and normalizes only CSV line endings;
two lanes match the tracked files from 202 declared inputs. The separate
[author-example numerical correspondence](../../docs/report/publication-replay/landclim-author-example-correspondence/README.md)
reproduces three generated files from 157 declared candidate and author-R
inputs. The [published-window uncertainty envelopes](../../docs/report/publication-replay/landclim-published-window-uncertainty/README.md)
reproduce three generated files from six tracked inputs. These receipts
establish output ownership for bounded English and cross-version comparisons;
they do not add Nordic LRA admissions, independent vegetation skill or a
significance result from envelope overlap.

Our source-bound vegetation results illustrate the boundary: the
[Färskesjön](../science/vegetation-reference/farskesjon/plot_support.py),
[Småland historical-map](../science/vegetation-reference/smaland-historical-maps/coverage.py)
and [published-figure](../science/vegetation-reference/southern-sweden-figure/compare.py)
producers and numeric outputs live beside the
[vegetation decision](../science/vegetation-reference.md). Their exact
source replays do not create an independent measured-vegetation pair for our
predictions. The current science report states that limit directly.

We keep private licensed inputs in the qualified source store and record
their acquisition conditions and content identities with the consuming
domain. The tracked report should explain a result and its missing inputs
without requiring the private bytes to be present. Historical run
directories, deletion receipts and archive-member inventories do not belong
in this reader library.
