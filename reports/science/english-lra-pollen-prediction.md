## Original uniform-start study and its retained comparisons

The remaining historical narrative and terminal verification block describe the original uniform-start, analytical-pilot, direct-pollen and shared-PPE studies on their original denominators. References to opening target pollen describe those consumers' post-freeze scoring access, not a globally unread source cohort. The numerical values and original refused outcomes are retained unchanged.


We asked whether a real REVEALS/LOVE reconstruction could produce held-out
pollen predictions from an admitted author source profile. We selected 15
English sites in the 1250–1750 BP source window. Their 31-target author pollen
counts total 20,732 NISP. We excluded `Filipendula` (159 NISP, 0.767%)
because its published 0.006 m/s fall speed falls outside the selected
optimized LOVE transport contract. We kept that excluded mass and the
original count denominator with the exact source hashes. Our resulting
**30-target closed composition** is neither whole pollen nor whole vegetation.

We used the published remaining PPE and fall speeds, the declared homogeneous
author-example basin and ring assumptions, deterministic REVEALS and
ring-taxon-preserving LOVE. We chose one uniform-start SLSQP search that keeps
local cover nonnegative and closed. Its limits were 300 iterations, 16,000
objective evaluations and a `1e-8` objective tolerance, with no convergence
waiver. Our initial 120-iteration full-fold run produced 149 of 195 training
products but no fold predictions because 46 training fits refused. We raised
the limit in response to those training failures before opening any target
pollen. This is a development comparison with our analytical and
differential-evolution attempts. The original R source uses a different
optimizer.

We held out one physical site per outer fold and recomputed regional REVEALS
and training-site LOVE from the remaining pollen. We opened target pollen only
after publishing and authenticating each prediction product. The predicted
vegetation comes from LRA; we have no independent measured vegetation for an
accuracy score. This English experiment does not admit Swedish or four-country
LRA inputs.

## Zero-compatible composition-aware spatial prediction

A separately declared Hellinger spatial predictor now evaluates the original

refusals. All 450 new candidates were predicted and all 15 outer folds scored.
Each fold selected 20 km over 100 km using its fourteen inner-training targets;
there were no exact loss ties. The smallest inner mean-Hellinger gap was
0.01773175815. This is a point-model development result on previously exposed
source data, with fixed nominal `gpm neutral` transport.

For training compositions p and normalized Gaussian distance weights w, define
`a_j = sum_i w_i sqrt(p_ij)` and `q_j = a_j² / sum_k a_k²`.
For exactly closed ideal inputs, weighted squared Hellinger loss is
`1 - sum_j a_j sqrt(q_j)`: Cauchy–Schwarz gives its global minimum at this q.
This normalized root mean preserves common exact zeros and can leave the
ordinary convex hull of the training compositions. It uses no pseudocount,
taxon deletion or hidden source-row renormalization. Source closure retains the
existing absolute 1e-9 floating-point admission. A positive Gaussian weight or
supported output component lost to numerical zero refuses; none of the 450
actual candidates triggered those guards. The fitted mean minimizes vegetation
Hellinger loss, while scale selection scores physically forwarded pollen.
These are distinct objectives.
