# Main papers behind the current scientific argument

This inventory identifies the papers needed to understand our regional/local pollen models, publication reproduction and vegetation-reference claims. Inclusion follows a concrete owned scientific claim. It is not a general literature survey, and inclusion does not mean that an article's data, methods or findings have been reproduced. The [machine inventory](paper-accounts.csv) records each citation, role, data release, canonical reader account, original finding, our evidence and unresolved verification.

The original published population, later software fixture, source-row extraction and present repository prediction are different evidence units. The canonical account shows a worked example with its actual denominator, explains that distinction and names the missing input or decision. Every independent reader exercise and qualified scientific review remains outstanding; a report or model-generated review does not supply either.

## Why other source papers are not main accounts here

For offline reading, the [standalone PDF library](../exports/papers/README.md)
provides these nine accounts with the same canonical text and source citations.
The library records exact source/PDF hashes, embedded-font checks and a model
review of every page; it does not supply independent scientific endorsement
or actual collaborator comprehension.

Original lake chronology and regional vegetation-history papers support their particular core dossiers and retain citations there. Productivity compilations and transport papers support named parameter/source profiles. Their inclusion as a parameter or contextual citation does not automatically create a new headline validation claim. The [source-specific method profile](publication-correspondence/author-method-profile.md), [source cohort account](source-cohorts.md) and [vegetation-reference decision](vegetation-reference.md) keep those roles visible.

HYDE country/gridded source records have their own [source account](hyde-cropland-source-account.md); they are modeled land-use context, rather than independent vegetation truth or a reproduced main-paper experiment. Animal/aDNA appendices and archaeology source publications remain in their [owned evidence accounts](../README.md), since they do not validate REVEALS/LOVE or pollen-to-vegetation accuracy. Conditional archaeological country scope remains conditional.

Expand this inventory when a new scientific conclusion depends on an additional primary paper; add its citation, role, actual source release and canonical account before using it as a headline. Preserve source-specific receipt and missing-data boundaries explicitly. The supplied main articles are examined; original realized numerical inputs and applicable qualified decisions remain separate from article availability and must support any expanded reproduction or validation claim.
