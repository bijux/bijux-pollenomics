# Scientific method transfer for the Bökesjön pilot

The literature supports explicitly conditional calculations and potential recent
benchmarks. It does not supply the missing qualified BOKE core, basin, chronology,
taxonomy or parameter decisions. [Pilot assessment](README.md).

White Horse Hill `WHH05` supplies a source-replay example: **92.49% modeled
Calluna** is a site-level REVEALS estimate on its 31-taxon axis, not LOVE, measured
vegetation or a Swedish observation. Agreement between the released author R and
our calculation establishes numerical correspondence. Its 150 m receiving radius
and 100 km regional extent do not establish BOKE's relevant source area of pollen
(RSAP). [Example trace](../../publication-correspondence/author-example-site-replay.md)
and [LandClim II methods](https://essd.copernicus.org/articles/14/1581/2022/).

The existing Swedish conditional calculation already uses neutral Gaussian-plume
transport, 3 m/s wind, circular basins, fully terrestrial homogeneous annuli,
center-relative 500/1000 m local support, a 100 km regional extent, fixed KRAG
background and the declared clip-and-renormalize rule. These remain assumptions.
The separate 1.5 ha/1.6 ha/80 m alternatives vary BOKE basin size while preserving
that profile; equivalent modern circles are not ancient outlines or uncertainty
bounds. Both modeled taxa are trees, so their closed composition cannot quantify
forest/open cover. [Existing calculation](../krageholm-regional-coupled/README.md)
and [basin source card](../../../../data/neotoma/raw/bokesjon-core-sources/basin-sources.json).

[Fyfe et al. (2013)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.quascirev.2013.05.014), Sections 2–5,
compare regional backgrounds from a large lake and smaller-site networks,
excluding the target from its own background. That exclusion is reusable;
model-versus-model agreement is not measured vegetation accuracy. Spatial bias,
repeated time windows and the paper's inclusion of bushy vegetation in openness
limit direct transfer to a Swedish forest/open estimand.

[Nielsen and Odgaard (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00334-010-0249-z) use Danish
historical-map calibration, grouped vegetation classes, regional soil partitions
and parameter covariance. Their negative-cover response enlarges RSAP until
estimates are nonnegative under a 0.1-SD criterion. This differs from our
user-directed clipping rule; retain signed diagnostics. Their grouped PPEs and
source-area estimates are not BOKE parameters. An AD 1800 map benchmark requires
calibration-site overlap and reference support checks before an independence claim.

[Poska et al. (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10980-018-0615-2) was reviewed at
abstract/metadata level only. Its recent Estonian maps and historical records
suggest a benchmark route, but no usable reference dataset was acquired here.
Four lakes are not 79 independent places. These sources cannot validate ancient
BOKE cover. Likewise, provisional discussion guidance that 0–1 cm may represent
roughly ten years requires a core-specific age model; it is not a universal rate.

The [2020 PPE compilation](https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-12-3515-2020) supplies
traceable alternatives, not automatic replacements: European Ulmus RPP is missing,
compiled-mean SE is not population SD, and cross-taxon covariance is unavailable.
Northern Hemisphere Ulmus cannot silently fill Europe. The current LandClim
parameters remain unchanged. [Parameter-source interpretation](../../../../data/validation/pollen-productivity/wieczorek-herzschuh-2020/README.md).

The user-approved tolerance is **10 absolute percentage points**, not relative
10 percent. Profile differences can be described against that sensitivity
criterion; cover accuracy requires an independently admitted matched observation.
Existing exposed development and literature exposure remain recorded. No reserved
confirmation outcomes were opened or relabeled untouched for this note.

## Scientific verification and limits

**Supported claim.** Specific numerical methods and recent-reference leads can inform conditional transfer.

**Unsupported claim.** Qualified BOKE applicability, ancient geometry or measured vegetation accuracy.

**Actual verification performed.** Read Fyfe and Nielsen–Odgaard methods, Poska's abstract and existing example/PPE accounts; no benchmark rerun.

**Remaining input or review.** Source-bound physical/parameter decisions and compatible independent reference files remain required. Resolver: qualified BOKE/KRAG source and scientific reviewers and a vegetation-reference owner. Reopen when: A scoped decision or compatible reference packet is supplied.

**Cause category.** assumption|comparability|data|qualified review

**Consequence.** Keep sensitivity, numerical replay and accuracy distinct.

**Precise next acceptance route.** Justify the selected profile and freeze compatible reference support and evaluation roles before testing accuracy.

**Negative or refused result.** No new measured-cover pair or full forest/open admission.

**Matched denominator.** Two newly retrieved full texts; one abstract-only paper; existing example/PPE accounts; zero new measured pairs.
Retained evidence: [existing source example](../../publication-correspondence/author-example-site-replay.md), [pilot source result](result.json), [Fyfe methods](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.quascirev.2013.05.014), [Danish methods](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00334-010-0249-z), [Estonian abstract](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10980-018-0615-2).

This account preserves the verification scope and source limitations; rendering these statements does not rerun or qualify the underlying science.
