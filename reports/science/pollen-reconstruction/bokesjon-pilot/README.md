# Bökesjön and Krageholm: pilot source assessment

**Public source identity, counts and parameter coverage are now checked. A full
forest/open/cereal reconstruction and measured vegetation accuracy remain open.**
The pilot examines Bökesjön (BOKE) with Krageholm (KRAG) regional background in
**2700–3200 calibrated years BP**, roughly 1250–750 BCE. It uses already exposed
development sources; it is not an untouched confirmation experiment.

## What the taxon check changes

The selected samples contain **2,553 reported pollen counts at BOKE** and
**3,973 at KRAG**. BOKE's omitted native cereal rows are distinct from the
private workbook's 12 explicit zero cells. The workbook's separate pollen total
is 2,556 and is not substituted for the public native denominator.

KRAG's two cereal labels and one sedge label, totaling **33 reported counts**,
lack an exact route in the retained author lookup. Its source dataset has no
separate rye label. These require explicit taxonomic and parameter decisions;
the audit applies no guessed mapping or zero imputation.

[Detailed source evidence and reproduction](methods.md) explains all counts,
zeros, chronology controls, parameter coverage, basin evidence and exclusions.

## What closes the pilot

1. Select and justify a complete taxon/denominator/PPE profile and its uncertainty.
2. Obtain a scoped decision on the core, chronology, static-basin assumptions
   and KRAG's regional applicability, then execute the admitted reconstruction.
3. Admit a compatible independent vegetation reference and freeze its evaluation
   protocol before confirmation outcomes.

The user confirms a **10-percentage-point absolute cover-error tolerance**.
It cannot be evaluated as accuracy here because no measured pair is admitted.
The British White Horse Hill example and Danish/Estonian studies can inform
explicit development assumptions; their model outputs are not Swedish observations.

**Verified:** 67 focused tests; an independent check of all 792 native slots,
29 dating controls, both DOI records and 35 consumed pins; zero count discrepancy.
The source audit called no LRA engine. [Result identity](result.json) ·
[Independent proof](independent-check.json) · [Taxon ledger](taxon-support.csv).

## Scientific verification and limits

**Supported claim.** The complete source-native BOKE/KRAG taxon window, public core/chronology/rights identities and literal parameter availability are source-bound and independently checked.

**Unsupported claim.** A scientifically admitted full forest/open/cereal reconstruction, observed ancient basin, calibrated age/parameter uncertainty, untouched confirmation or measured vegetation accuracy.

**Actual verification performed.** 67 focused tests; producer 13.37 s / 176 MB and independent observer 14.23 s / 107 MB; all 35 pins, 792 native slots, 29 controls, 2 DOIs, 31 LandClim targets, 110 European reference rows and private workbook cells checked; 20,646 scalar comparisons, maximum count error 0.

**Remaining input or review.** Qualified taxon/denominator/parameter and static-basin/regional applicability decisions remain absent; reviewed sources supply no matched ancient non-pollen cover vector. Resolver: BOKE/KRAG source custodians, qualified palynology/chronology/basin reviewers and an independent vegetation-reference owner. Reopen when: A scoped justified source/profile decision or compatible independent measured cover packet is supplied.

**Cause category.** assumption|comparability|data|qualified review

**Consequence.** Keep all omissions, source denominators, unresolved cereal/rye mapping and missing uncertainty/refusal states distinct; do not score vegetation accuracy.

**Precise next acceptance route.** Select and justify the complete taxon/parameter profile and physical assumptions, carry the admitted inputs through the maintained LRA workflow, then evaluate a separately frozen compatible independent reference.

**Negative or refused result.** No full 31-taxon or rye-split admission; KRAG 33 reported NISP lack candidate lookup routes; no admitted measured vegetation pair; missing source counts are not zero-filled.

**Matched denominator.** 148 source samples: 52 BOKE + 96 KRAG; 7 selected samples: 3 + 4; 792 taxon/sample slots: 218 reported positive + 574 omitted candidates; reported NISP 2,553 + 3,973. Workbook 2,556 and 12 explicit crop-zero cells remain separate.
