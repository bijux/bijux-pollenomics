# Bökesjön and Krageholm: pilot source assessment

**Public source identity, counts and parameter coverage are now checked. A full
forest/open/cereal reconstruction and measured vegetation accuracy remain open.**
The pilot examines Bökesjön (BOKE) with Krageholm (KRAG) regional background in
**2700–3200 calibrated years BP**, roughly 1250–750 BCE. It uses already exposed
development sources; it is not an untouched confirmation experiment.

## What the taxon check changes

The selected samples contain **2,553 reported pollen counts at BOKE** and
**3,973 at KRAG**. BOKE's omitted native cereal rows are distinct from the
private workbook's 12 explicit zero cells. The workbook's separate pollen total
is 2,556 and is not substituted for the public native denominator.

KRAG's two cereal labels and one sedge label, totaling **33 reported counts**,
lack an exact route in the retained author lookup. Its source dataset has no
separate rye label. These require explicit taxonomic and parameter decisions;
the audit applies no guessed mapping or zero imputation. A later metadata-only
follow-up identifies a Cyperaceae family-parent candidate for the three sedge
counts, with aquatic/terrestrial eligibility still unresolved. The 30 cereal
counts remain without a rye-compatible definition. [Taxon-definition evidence](taxon-definitions.md).

[Detailed source evidence and reproduction](methods.md) explains all counts,
zeros, chronology controls, parameter coverage, basin evidence and exclusions.

## What the basin check shows

Three fresh reconstructions compare modern 1.5 ha, modern 1.6 ha and metadata
80 m radius profiles. Their outer-ring Betula–Ulmus fractions span **3.03
percentage points**. Inner-ring signed fractions span **8.47 points**, while
clipping makes all three Betula fractions zero. That zero is not evidence of
vegetation absence. This is conditional two-tree sensitivity, not forest/open
cover or measured accuracy. [Results, assumptions and independent proof](basin-sensitivity/README.md).

## What the nominal PPE comparison shows

The maintained two-case comparison changes shared Betula PPE from **5.106 to
4.94**, keeping Ulmus at 1.27 because the selected European reference has no
Ulmus RPP. Source SD metadata, fall speeds, pooled counts, chronology and physical
assumptions remain fixed; no source-SD law or weighted uncertainty is inferred.

Outer-ring adjusted Betula increases by **1.233837 percentage points**. Inner-ring
signed Betula increases by **1.587917 points**, while both adjusted Betula values
remain zero after per-case clipping. That masked change is not evidence of taxon
absence or parameter irrelevance. The selected two-tree panel is not full native
pollen NISP or forest/open/cereal cover. Full/excluded native denominators are not
bound to these saved pooled-bin requests; the separately audited 2,553 BOKE and
3,973 KRAG totals above remain distinct, without an inferred allocation or excluded
mass. The retained **100 km** regional extent differs from the 2025
paper's **1000 km** extent; this is not paper reproduction or measured accuracy.

The [conditional nominal comparison account](parameter-sensitivity/README.md) has
[separate output qualification](parameter-sensitivity/verification.json). Two model
cases and publication completed, but the original operator subsequently refused
during a late software-identity bootstrap check and has no successful terminal
receipt. Its failure remains immutable; read-only output and parameter-lineage
checks qualify the saved result separately, without rerunning models. Original
sources and full child bundles remain private.

## Scientific verification and limits

**Supported claim.** The complete source-native BOKE/KRAG taxon window, public core/chronology/rights identities and literal parameter availability are independently checked; three declared static-basin profiles have a checked conditional two-tree sensitivity result.

**Unsupported claim.** A scientifically admitted full forest/open/cereal reconstruction, observed ancient basin, calibrated age/parameter uncertainty, untouched confirmation or measured vegetation accuracy.

**Actual verification performed.** 93 focused pilot tests; producer 13.37 s / 176 MB and independent observer 14.23 s / 107 MB; all 35 pins, 792 native slots, 29 controls, 2 DOIs, 31 LandClim targets, 110 European reference rows and private workbook cells checked; 20,646 scalar comparisons, maximum count error 0.

**Remaining input or review.** Qualified taxon/denominator/parameter and static-basin/regional applicability decisions remain absent; reviewed sources supply no matched ancient non-pollen cover vector. Resolver: BOKE/KRAG source custodians, qualified palynology/chronology/basin reviewers and an independent vegetation-reference owner. Reopen when: A scoped justified source/profile decision or compatible independent measured cover packet is supplied.

**Cause category.** assumption|comparability|data|qualified review

**Consequence.** Keep all omissions, source denominators, unresolved cereal/rye mapping and missing uncertainty/refusal states distinct; do not score vegetation accuracy.

**Precise next acceptance route.** Select and justify the complete taxon/parameter profile and physical assumptions, carry the admitted inputs through the maintained LRA workflow, then evaluate a separately frozen compatible independent reference.

**Negative or refused result.** No full 31-taxon or rye-split admission; KRAG 33 reported NISP lack exact author-lookup routes; no admitted measured vegetation pair; missing source counts are not zero-filled.

**Matched denominator.** 148 source samples: 52 BOKE + 96 KRAG; 7 selected samples: 3 + 4; 792 taxon/sample slots: 218 reported positive + 574 omitted candidates; reported NISP 2,553 + 3,973. Workbook 2,556 and 12 explicit crop-zero cells remain separate.
