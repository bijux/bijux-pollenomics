# Scientific evidence by Nordic country

The current four-country result asks whether observed pollen composition transfers to a country excluded from training. It predicts the **31 non-excluded targets** in the LandClim II author taxonomy lookup, using selected Neotoma/EPD pollen counts in **500-cal-BP site-bins**. Three fixed baselines predict each eligible bin; a bin without the required training-site support is refused. The [machine-readable matrix](country-evidence-status.csv) retains the native pollen count mass, assigned mass, excluded and unmapped mass, attempted and scored bins, and exact result paths. These are previously exposed **development** cohorts, not untouched confirmation, REVEALS/LOVE reconstructions or measured vegetation tests. The [cohort account](source-cohorts.md) and separate [Denmark/Finland/Norway](denmark-finland-norway-author-pollen.md) and [Sweden](sweden-author-pollen.md) accounts explain the selection and scoring rules.

Across these declared populations, **1,594 of 1,691 attempted site-bins** were scored, **97** refused, and the scored bins yield **4,782 method rows** across three fixed methods. These sums describe a shared 31-target response, not a pooled four-country accuracy estimate. Counts outside the 31 targets remain visible in the [taxonomy ledger](../../docs/report/regions/nordic/author-taxonomy-mass/sites.csv) and the [Swedish result](../../docs/report/regions/nordic/swedish-author-target-pollen/report.json). The earlier **16-label** analysis has another response denominator; its scores cannot be compared numerically as if the axes were identical. Country exclusion and frozen predictions support this bounded development comparison, while earlier exposure of the Nordic outcomes prevents an independent confirmation claim.
