## How to read the limits

A source-reported zero, a governed assignment, and a published member can all coexist. SEAD reports zero country cells in its native source partition while governed and published site assignments are nonzero. EMPD2 source metadata is partial, yet its governed country assignment and publication remain blocked. HYDE private archive custody does not satisfy the governed repository or country projection contracts. NFI country access leads do not become admitted pollen–vegetation pairs.

For lifecycle stages and source roles, see the [source-family matrix](source-family-matrix.md). For count definitions and denied inferences, see [coverage and naming](../overview/coverage-and-naming.md). The [retained replay](../../../report/regions/nordic/source-capability/README.md) includes all six partitions and parent-artifact identities.
