# What the evidence supports in each Nordic country

The current country comparisons predict observed pollen composition on a
31-target response. They are development studies whose outcomes were
previously exposed. A country appearing on the map or in a score table does
not establish country-wide LRA reconstruction, independent vegetation
accuracy or untouched confirmation.

The [DK/FI/NO result](../../../report/regions/nordic/author-target-pollen/README.md)
and [Swedish result](../../../report/regions/nordic/swedish-author-target-pollen/README.md)
own these denominators. Every bin is 500 cal BP wide. Country exclusion removes
target-country sites from training before prediction. Denmark's single target
site cannot establish between-site Danish performance. Refused Norwegian and
Swedish bins remain visible; they are not zero-error predictions.

These cohorts admit **zero samples to LRA**. Each proposed core still needs its
selected chronology, dated basin geometry, eligible count/taxon denominator,
parameter version and permitted use together. The [source admission account](sources.md#before-a-core-can-enter-lra)
explains the unresolved named-core fields. The separate English LRA result and
author-R numerical comparisons do not supply those Nordic inputs.

No matched independent measured-vegetation benchmark has been admitted for
these country claims. [Measured vegetation evidence](results.md#measured-vegetation-evidence)
explains the pairing refusals. HYDE cropland grids and LandClim map estimates
are modeled context, with their own source and time conventions; they are not
independent observations of the pollen targets. Follow the [atlas walkthrough](../interfaces/atlas-walkthrough.md)
to inspect one such context cell and reproduce its bounded panel summary.

The [scientific results](results.md) keep age-support sensitivities, numerical
correspondence and model comparisons separate. Qualified source, boundary and
scientific review decisions remain required. This status table describes the
local evidence candidate; it does not assert an accepted four-country
scientific release or deployed public availability.
