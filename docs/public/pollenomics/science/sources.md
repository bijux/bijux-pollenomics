# Scientific sources and scope

The [four-country author-taxonomy study](../../../report/regions/nordic/author-target-pollen/README.md)
withholds one target country from its training set, assigns native pollen
counts to 31 author targets and preserves all excluded or unmapped count mass.
Its [input manifest](../../../report/regions/nordic/author-target-pollen/input-manifest.json),
[coverage](../../../report/regions/nordic/author-target-pollen/coverage.csv) and
[score table](../../../report/regions/nordic/author-target-pollen/scores.csv)
identify the exact support.

| Target | Sites | Samples | Assigned/native pollen NISP | Scored/attempted bins |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Denmark | 1 | 91 | 122,998/129,768 | 24/24 |
| Finland | 28 | 1,797 | 1,229,019/1,260,281 | 435/435 |
| Norway | 52 | 3,544 | 2,018,295/2,286,020 | 1,084/1,178 |
| Sweden | 3 | 228 | 221,800/240,966 | 51/54 |

The 94 Norwegian and three Swedish unsupported bins remain explicit
refusals. Denmark's one site cannot establish between-site Danish skill.
Country outcomes had already been inspected in earlier development, so these
are exploratory scores. The response is a closed 31-target pollen
subcomposition, not full pollen, modeled vegetation or measured vegetation.
Its MAE should not be compared directly with the earlier 16-label response.

## Before a core can enter LRA

The physical core, selected age model, age-specific basin geometry, count
denominator, mapped taxon concepts, PPE/fall speed use and rights must refer
to the same analytical window. The [Abborrtjärnen source review](../../../report/source-relations/neotoma/abbo-published-method/README.md)
matches substantial depth and count evidence but cannot yet identify the
LandClim-selected chronology or historical basin series. The
[Solsø source-lineage review](../../../report/countries/denmark/solsoe-source-lineage/README.md)
retains a different sample-selection question: LandClim II names 79 pollen
samples, while the Neotoma site has 92 source samples and its published age
span alone retains 90. The Nordic observed-pollen
scores do not bypass those gates.

The [HYDE source result](../../../report/regions/nordic/hyde-country-cropland/README.md)
checks modeled cropland quantity accounting at selected labels. It is not an
observed vegetation series. The [vegetation reference review](../../../report/validation/empd2-nfi-pairing/README.md)
currently admits zero matched measurement pairs for accuracy scoring.

## A named lake and nearby archaeology

We resolved the proposed Bjäresjösjön name to the modern official Bjäresjö
lake registry record. The [source screen](../../../report/countries/sweden/bjaresjo-context-screen/README.md)
finds 371 SEAD display intervals from 13 sites within 20 km and reproduces
all 25 existing modeled-window context counts. The nearby Neotoma pollen
site and LandClim `BJARE` sequence remain different, unconfirmed core
identities. SEAD chronology remains display context without qualified event
propagation. We have therefore withheld a lake-specific ecological join,
archaeology weighting and LRA basin transfer.
