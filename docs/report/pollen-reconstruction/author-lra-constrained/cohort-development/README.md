# Retrospective English LOVE development cohort

This homogeneous retrospective diagnostic applies one frozen fixed-start LOVE method to all 15 previously exposed English author sites. It preserves the original uniform-start evaluation as a separate method and retains that evaluation’s refused BECKHAM fold. These observations describe a development cohort; prediction freezing does not make its previously seen outcomes untouched confirmation.

The admitted run retains **1588 accepted and 140 refused attempts out of 1,728 unique attempts**: 896 newly executed and 832 authenticated prior attempts. The 195 fold-specific training requests reuse 27 unique numerical requests, producing 12,480 logical attempt references. A logical reference is reuse of an existing attempt, not another fit, site, independent observation or uncertainty draw.

The complete inventory contains **45 prediction or refusal slots** across 15 folds and three predeclared spatial baselines. **15 folds were predicted and 0 refused**. Every refused ring, failed fold and unavailable score remains explicit in the retained products. The table reports only scored folds; omitted score rows do not become measured zeros.

Each fold excludes its own target from training and from optimizer-start selection. A target legitimately trains other folds. All 45 prediction or refusal slots were written and authenticated again before any new cohort scoring. This is an operational ordering boundary, not a claim that the source targets were globally unread or scientifically unexposed.

Each scored fold receives equal weight in this descriptive summary. Means, medians and ranges are point diagnostics, not inferential intervals or independent-sample estimates. Refused folds are excluded from score summaries and remain in the separate fold denominator.
