# Grouped author-pollen development search

The search attempted 12 candidates over three deterministic, grid-stratified whole-site folds, with 184 observed target site-bin attempts and 174 common scored site-bins. The selected candidate was `clr_rbf_q25_ridge_0.1` at common-support site-macro MAE 0.017774 fraction and 94.6% own-support coverage.

The three folds test held-out author site IDs with nearby-site interpolation. The English author-prepared outcomes were previously opened, and these results are development evidence only. Two adjacent grids, unreviewed cross-database aliases, prepared 31-taxon counts and absent independent vegetation truth limit generalization and ecological claims. This search does not establish Swedish LRA readiness or measured vegetation accuracy.
