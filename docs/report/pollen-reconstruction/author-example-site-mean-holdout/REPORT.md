# Author-example observed pollen site holdouts

The authenticated author-prepared 31-taxon count tables provide 17 site IDs in two grids. Each site ID was withheld in turn. For a target bin with counts, the predictor is the equal-site mean of observed pollen fractions from other site IDs in that grid and bin. The installed pollen scorer compares that prediction with withheld author-prepared integer counts. Target counts never enter their own fit. Zero-count bins are unavailable, not zero-valued observations.

Of 184 observed site-bin targets, 177 were scored and 7 had no other site in the bin. The mean of the 17 site-level mean absolute errors is 0.018949 in fraction units; site-macro total variation is 0.293703. The CSVs retain each bin score, refusal and taxon prediction.

This is an exploratory observed-pollen baseline on author examples, not a Swedish cohort, spatial-block evaluation, LRA reconstruction score, or measured vegetation accuracy. Author site IDs have no reviewed cross-database alias closure; bins within a site are correlated and are summarized within site before the macro average. The 31 author-prepared taxa do not certify a full raw-core pollen denominator or source reuse rights for publication. No tuned parameters or uncertainty calibration are claimed.
