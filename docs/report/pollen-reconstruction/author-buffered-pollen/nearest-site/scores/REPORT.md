# Buffered adjacent-grid pollen prediction

Two one-degree author grids are withheld in turn. Prediction reads only the opposite grid's count tables and predicts each author bin with nearest_site_31_taxon_observed_pollen_fraction_by_bin. A training basin must be at least 20 km from every target basin after subtracting both reported basin radii. The independently reopened target counts are scored afterward.

Across 17 author site IDs and 2 adjacent blocks, 184 site-bin targets have observed counts, 148 are scored and 36 have no buffered same-bin training site. Site-macro MAE is 0.025285 fractions; site-macro total variation is 0.391916. On 148 site bins scored by both methods, the site-macro MAE is 0.025285 for the buffered block holdout and 0.019518 for the retained within-grid site holdout. This paired difference is descriptive development evidence; coverage differs between methods.

This is a development comparison on author-prepared pollen, not a Swedish cohort, LRA vegetation reconstruction, or measured vegetation accuracy. The 20 km basin-edge buffer is a declared diagnostic rule; the broader pollen source footprints and cross-database site aliases are not independently closed. Two adjacent blocks cannot establish general spatial-transfer uncertainty. Author-prepared 31-taxon counts do not certify a full raw-core pollen denominator or publication rights.
