# ESSD published-window uncertainty envelopes

The pinned 2016 author-R replay and PANGAEA 937075 June 2021 product are paired for 31 taxa in each of grids 512 and 562 at 100–350 BP. Each envelope is the reported mean ± one reported standard error, in percentage cover. Two envelopes are disjoint when the absolute mean gap exceeds the sum of their standard errors.

This is a descriptive comparison of two reported uncertainty scales. Their cross-version dependence and historical raw-input identity are unknown, so envelope overlap is not a significance test. The result does not measure vegetation accuracy or reproduce the full study.
