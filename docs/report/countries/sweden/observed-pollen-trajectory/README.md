## Distance sensitivity of nearby source features

We compared the retained spherical distance with WGS84 for all 8,799 source features at the BUS and BOKE points and the declared alternative BUS point. The [aggregate report](geodesic-sensitivity.json) and [window ledger](geodesic-window-cells.csv) preserve all 156 method/window cells. Of these features,7,244 have numeric date support and 1,555 remain unresolved.

Twenty-nine spatial memberships change. Only the BOKE 20 km windows 0–500 and 500–1,000 BP change their dated context: each loses 14 features from one site, with 18 and 16 fewer distinct source claims respectively. The minimum WGS84 boundary margin is 4.755 metres; no feature is within 1 mm. This changes source proximity support; it does not establish archaeological event age, vegetation or causal land use.

The [source account](geodesic-source.json) preserves native execution, all 78 reproduced original spherical cells, reference controls and failed preparations. WGS84 checks share the PROJ backend; they are not a second ellipsoid implementation. Source chronology, uncertainty qualifiers and the shared LandClim grid remain unchanged.

## Counting uncertainty on the literal pollen pair

A separate [counting-only pair trajectory](pair-counting.json) uses explicit native **Alnus** and **Plantago lanceolata** NISP pollen counts. It retains 37 of 69 BUS aliquots and 43 of 52 BOKE aliquots: 80 of 121 original source objects, with 13,415 measured pair grains. The error-allocation family includes all 121 source objects before selection. Missing native datum rows remain unresolved; 21 BUS objects lie outside the fixed bins, and missing-axis and outside-bin refusal reasons overlap. The report exposes every population and refusal count; these 80 aliquots represent the admitted pair subset rather than either complete pollen assemblage.

Individual tail-certified intervals use fixed response and PPE values [1,1], exact count-weighted aggregation and the prespecified Bonferroni allocation of at most 1/20 family error under the declared conditional binomial model. This calculation assumes neither a shared probability among aliquots nor independence for the union bound. It establishes no nominal coverage conditional on source-dependent admission, no random-total sampling design and no biological representativeness. Native point chronology is held fixed; age uncertainty is outside this calculation.

Of 26 fixed lake/bin slots, 23 contain admitted observations. The 24 adjacent Alnus pair-probability contrasts comprise one strictly positive, 19 indeterminate and four unavailable outcomes. Here every contrast runs **from the younger bin to the older bin, with increasing calendar BP**. BUS 2500–3000 → 3000–3500 BP has a pair-probability difference 0.243809 and simultaneous bound [0.127105,0.356960]; this is an older-minus-younger pair contrast, not evidence of forward-time ecological expansion. No negative contrast is resolved. All 12 paired lake-direction relations remain unsupported, including the one for which BUS is resolved and BOKE is indeterminate.

The [source and verification account](pair-counting-source.json) records the actual 44 focused guards and independent reconstruction of 121 source objects, 80 intervals, 26 bins and 24 contrasts. A separate directed 110 digit positive combinatorial sum checked each actual interval endpoint; the maintained general API proof is reused. Detailed aliquots, native source pointers, request logs and per-aliquot certificates remain private. This estimand and its uncertainty family differ from the earlier 48 all-NISP/closed 16 taxon threshold classes; it does not recertify those classes or establish physical LRA, vegetation/land-use accuracy, independent validation or rights approval.

## Scientific verification and limits

Swedish observed pollen/modeled land use/archaeological context has separate provenance and no causal or ecological validation.

**Supported claim.** The retained two-lake trajectory is supplemented by an actual source-bound spherical/WGS84 context-membership sensitivity: 8,799 features, three prespecified coordinates and 156 method/window cells. Twenty-nine spatial memberships differ; only two dated BOKE 20 km bins change. Separate counting scenario: The separate literal Alnus/Plantago lanceolata counting scenario resolves one BUS older-minus-younger conditional pair-probability contrast; all twelve paired lake-direction relations remain unsupported. Native point chronology is fixed.

**Unsupported claim.** The pointer and descriptive two-lake agreement cannot validate land use or vegetation: LandClim shares these input sites and SEAD lacks a qualified comparable ecological measurement. Separate counting scenario: The admitted two-taxon subset and fixed response/PPE scenario do not establish ecological, total-vegetation, physical LRA, land-use or independent validation claims, nominal coverage conditional on source-dependent selection, a random-total sampling design, or recertification of the original 48 all-NISP/closed16 threshold classes.

**Actual verification performed.** Actual 26,397 feature–centre distance comparisons reproduced all 78 original sphere cells. Independent source/ID/bin/count reconstruction and scalar distance checks passed; 57 published distance/boundary controls retained. Two public aggregate products exactly reproduce qualified private outputs; no source chronology or pollen model was refitted. Separate counting scenario: The actual source consumer passed 44 focused guards. Its separate observer reconstructed all 121 native objects, 80 intervals, 26 bins and 24 adjacent contrasts, using exact Fraction aggregation and a directed 110 digit positive combinatorial tail sum at every nontrivial endpoint. General API proof is reused; publication projects authenticated aggregates without new interval fits or source-count analysis.

**Remaining input or review.** Qualified independent ecological measurements and source-compatible chronology are required before interpreting concordance as a land-use trajectory. Separate counting scenario: Source sampling-design/selection qualification, accepted response/PPE uncertainty, compatible age uncertainty and independent ecological observations are still required for broader claims; literal source absences remain unresolved.

**Cause category.** Citation routing to descriptive observed-pollen trajectories with dependent modeled context. Separate counting scenario: Counting-only conditional pair inference on an explicitly measured admitted source subset, with source-dependent missing axes and fixed native point chronology.

**Consequence.** Apparent agreement or contradiction remains source-local description; no independent land-use/vegetation conclusion follows.
