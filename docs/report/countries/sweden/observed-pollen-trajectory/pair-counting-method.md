## Scientific verification and limits

This methods page defines a separate fixed-count conditional pollen-pair estimand. It preserves the distinction from historical pollen percentages, physical LRA and ecological accuracy.

**Supported claim.** Prespecified all-source family allocation and exact count-weighted individual intervals define a conditional Alnus/Plantago lanceolata pair comparison at fixed native point ages. The finite observed scenario resolves one older-minus-younger BUS contrast.

**Unsupported claim.** This selected pollen pair does not establish total vegetation, physical LRA, ecological or land-use accuracy, nominal coverage conditional on source-dependent admission, or a random-total sampling design. Original 48 threshold classes are not recertified.

**Actual verification performed.** Actual native consumer passed 44 focused guards; independent reconstruction covered 121 source objects, 80 returned intervals, 26 bins and 24 contrasts. Directed 110 digit positive combinatorial tails checked every nontrivial endpoint. Canonical 72 guards, strict12 and configured lint/format passed; two maintained configured commands reproduce the authenticated aggregate bytes without new fits.

**Remaining input or review.** Sampling-design and selection qualification, compatible age uncertainty, response/PPE uncertainty and independent ecological observations remain required for broader scientific use. External qualified review and intended-use rights remain open.

**Cause category.** Fixed conditional counting model, fixed response/PPE values, fixed native point chronology and source-dependent incomplete axes.

**Consequence.** The method has a finite conditional estimand. Its positive direction runs toward older BP bins; neither that direction nor repeat measurements establish forward-time ecological expansion or independent sites.

**Precise next acceptance route.** Inspect the aggregate report, source account and this original method together. Qualify source sampling and chronology before broadening the estimator or its scientific interpretation.

**Negative or refused result.** Nineteen adjacent contrasts are indeterminate and four unavailable; no negative contrast is resolved, and all twelve paired lake-direction relations remain unsupported. Missing native axes remain unresolved.

**Matched denominator.** All-source family 121 objects: BUS 69 and BOKE 52. Admitted subset 80: 37 and 43, with 13,415 pair grains; 23 occupied of 26 lake/bin slots, 24 contrasts and 12 cross-lake relations. Refusal reasons overlap.
