# Swedish cross-source site comparison

The pinned LandClim II metadata has 84 Swedish sequences; the retained Neotoma prerequisite projection has 77 Swedish candidate sites, 68 with point coordinates. 34 have a same-name published sequence with comparable point coordinates; 16 are within 10 m. These are identity review leads, not admitted cross-source joins.

For Klotjärnen, Neotoma site 13336 is at (16.40472, 61.82125) and altitude 214 m. LandClim II sequence KLO (workbook row 1003) documents a 56 m site radius but is 6.773 km away. The radius cannot be transferred to the Neotoma site from its name.

The workbook defines `siteradius` as metres; the source receipt authenticates the workbook. Site names and coordinates do not establish matching cores, source rights, chronological support or pollen-count denominators. No LRA samples are admitted by this comparison. `site-comparison.csv` retains each source lead and the refusal status.
