\begin{frame}{The question: what grew around a dated sediment core?}
\begin{itemize}
\item Sediment preserves grains from plants near and far. Their counts record pollen; we want to understand vegetation and landscape change.
\item A useful result must identify the physical core, sample age, counted taxa and the landscape area represented. Those decisions determine whether past forest or farming change can be interpreted.
\item The project traces those inputs, checks selected published calculations and tests predictions on declared pollen targets.
\end{itemize}
\scope{We currently have observed-pollen prediction and bounded calculation evidence. We have no independent measured-vegetation accuracy score for our Nordic predictions.}
\evidence{reports/science/source-cohorts.md; reports/science/vegetation-reference.md}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Grain share and land-cover share are different quantities}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{p{.40\linewidth}p{.47\linewidth}}
\toprule
What we observe & What a model tries to infer \\
\midrule
Pollen fraction: a taxon's grains divided by a declared grain count. & Vegetation fraction: a taxon's cover divided by a declared landscape area. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Plants produce unequal quantities of pollen; grains travel and settle differently. A lake's size and type affect the incoming signal.
\scope{Productivity and transport corrections connect these quantities under assumptions. A grain percentage alone is not a measured vegetation percentage.}
\evidence{reports/science/landclim-paper.md; reports/science/loveoptim-paper.md}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Regional and local reconstruction need different inputs}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{REVEALS}: Regional Estimates of VEgetation Abundance from Large Sites. Large-site pollen informs regional cover under declared transport and basin assumptions.
\item \textbf{LOVE}: LOcal Vegetation Estimates. Regional background and small-site pollen inform cover in a defined local area.
\item Together they form the \textbf{Landscape Reconstruction Algorithm (LRA)}. Relative pollen productivity estimates (PPEs), fall speed, basin geometry and selected ages are inputs, not optional annotations.
\end{itemize}
\scope{A working implementation does not admit a new historical core. Its source identity, parameters, geometry, rights and physical interpretation need evidence.}
\evidence{reports/science/reveals-theory-paper.md; reports/science/love-theory-paper.md}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{A concrete source-to-result example: one lake sample}
\begin{enumerate}
\item White Horse Hill (\texttt{WHH05}), depth 16: source age 1,320 calibrated years before 1950, with 301 grains in named source fields.
\item Four explicitly excluded grains leave 297 on the author's declared axis. The age enters [1,250, 1,750), labelled 1,500 years BP.
\item On this prepared example, author R and our implementation give modeled \textit{Calluna} cover 0.924883 and standard error 0.046807, in fraction units.
\end{enumerate}
\scope{The interval label is not a new date. This is a modeled site value, not 92.49\% of the grains or measured vegetation. Numerical agreement checks this calculation.}
\evidence{reports/science/landclim-paper.md; reports/science/publication-correspondence/author-example-site-replay.md}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Age support can change which observations are scored}
\begin{itemize}
\item At S\"ambosj\"on, the 16-label observed-pollen study scored 22 bins from 111 samples. Source age intervals cross bin boundaries for 58 samples.
\item Keeping only contained intervals leaves 53 samples and 21 bins. On identical predictions, primary MAE is 0.023054 versus 0.022881 for original counts on those same bins.
\item One bin has no contained sample and retains refused, null scores. Retained samples contribute 185,150 native grains, including 108,163 on the 16-label response.
\end{itemize}
\scope{One exposed core; the source bounds have no stated confidence level or joint age posterior. This sensitivity does not establish independent confirmation or vegetation accuracy.}
\evidence{reports/science/sambosjon-direct-pollen.md; docs/report/regions/nordic/sambosjon-direct-pollen/interval-support/report.json}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{English LRA: coverage and predictive quality are separate}
\small
\begin{itemize}
\item The original uniform-start study used 15 author sites and 30 pollen targets. Fourteen sites supplied all three predictions; BECKHAM refused.
\item Later fixed-start training supplied 45 predictions on all 15 exposed TW5 development sites. Recovering a refused fold did not establish lower error: the matched 14-site comparison was slightly worse for all three baselines.
\item All sixteen declared settings now retain outcomes, including failures, aliases and separate time windows. They are not sixteen successful or directly comparable models.
\end{itemize}
\scope{Observed-pollen development evidence, not measured-vegetation accuracy. The following direct-versus-LRA table retains the original matched 14-site uniform-start study.}
\evidence{reports/science/english-lra-pollen-prediction.md}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Matching author code does not reproduce the whole archive}
\begin{itemize}
\item Selected LandClim examples: author R and our implementation agree on 14,074 supported means and standard errors. The largest fraction differences are $1.84\times10^{-13}$ and $7.16\times10^{-14}$.
\item Only one of 25 time windows has identical boundaries in example and archive: 100--350 BP. Archived mean cover differs by up to 9.20 and 10.55 percentage points in the two grids.
\item The released example has 17 sites; the paper selected 1,128 historical records. Their original input revisions and archive functional-type transformations remain unverified here.
\end{itemize}
\scope{The archive gap is retained, but its cause is not identified. Numerical parity, historical source identity and ecological accuracy are separate questions.}
\evidence{reports/science/landclim-paper.md; reports/science/publication-correspondence/published-window-provenance.md}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Published vegetation references have useful limits}
\begin{itemize}
\item Historical Sm\aa land maps provide coverage and pollen anchors; the numerical class-area fractions needed for a new score are unavailable.
\item A southern Swedish published figure supports nine rounded label comparisons. It does not recover all original taxa or score our model.
\item Namibian observations join ten soil-pollen sites to 15 vegetation-radius labels. Repeated radius rows are not new pollen samples, and the population differs from Nordic lakes.
\end{itemize}
\scope{Our Nordic plot/pollen screen admits zero of 16 nearby leads. None of these distinct sources supplies independent measured cover for our current Nordic predictions.}
\evidence{reports/science/vegetation-reference.md; reports/science/paper-accounts.md}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{What collaborators can inspect and decide}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Read the canonical paper account: question, concepts, worked example, original findings and our separate result.
\item Follow its exact table and source identities; use the reproduction route with lawful source access.
\item Help resolve a named core/age/basin/count/parameter question, historical archive transformation, or matched vegetation reference.
\item Review whether assumptions and spatial/time support justify the intended scientific conclusion. Actual reader feedback and qualified decisions remain open.
\end{enumerate}
\scope{Local PDFs and source are review material. Scientific review, publication authority and a complete release certificate are not supplied by a successful build.}
\evidence{reports/science/paper-accounts.md; reports/needed/README.md; docs/public/pollenomics/science/papers.md}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{What the new checks can establish}
\begin{itemize}
\item A grouped development experiment writes its split before fitting. Changing its held-out pollen and truth changes scores, while five target predictions and their training inputs stay fixed.
\item Two declared Krageholm basin sizes reuse the same counts and PPE. Their conditional paired errors retain that dependence; treating them as independent changes the uncertainty question.
\item An exposed English development ledger preserves all attempted fits and refusals. Freezing a new audit does not make an old fitted cohort untouched.
\end{itemize}
\scope{These are bounded software and conditional model checks. They supply no independent Nordic vegetation observations, historical basin authentication or qualified scientific review.}
\evidence{reports/science/pollen-prediction.md; docs/report/pollen-reconstruction/krageholm-static-geometry-sensitivity/README.md; docs/report/model-development/english-retained-score-audit/README.md}
\end{frame}
