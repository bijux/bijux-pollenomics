\begin{frame}{Swedish training scope on shared pollen targets}
\begin{itemize}
\item Hold out each Swedish site; train on the other two Swedish sites or those same two plus 81 DK/FI/NO sites.
\item On 39 shared site-bins, inverse-distance-squared equal-site pollen MAE is 0.032846 versus 0.023226, favoring four-country training.
\item Sweden-only refuses 15 of 54 candidate bins; four-country training predicts all 54.
\end{itemize}
\scope{Three previously exposed sites; most mean gain comes from Abborrtjarnen. No LRA or independent vegetation accuracy follows.}
\evidence{reports/science/sweden-training-scope.md; docs/report/regions/nordic/swedish-training-scope/paired-scores.csv}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Swedish source support changes the pollen score}
\begin{itemize}
\item We froze 1,419 method predictions across 473 supported queries; 13 of 486 site-bin/policy queries refused.
\item Adjacent nominal 500-year source bins and a nearest-five cap changed IDW error slightly and differently by site.
\item Excluding source centers within 100 km raised matched equal-site IDW MAE from 0.023151 to 0.029171 on 52 bins.
\end{itemize}
\scope{Three exposed Swedish sites; site centers, no age posterior, LRA reconstruction or measured vegetation.}
\evidence{reports/science/sweden-spatiotemporal-sensitivity.md; docs/report/regions/nordic/swedish-support-sensitivity/paired-scores.csv}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{What reproduces numerically}
\begin{itemize}
\item Author raw-to-prepared replay: 17 example files, 871 raw sample rows, 15,028 matching prepared cells.
\item WHH05: 301 raw grains map to 297 prepared; 31 paired means and SEs agree at 1500 BP.
\item Reexecuted author R/native calculations on two grids (9+8 sites); 14,074 supported mean and SE values agree within declared tolerances.
\item Archived 100--350 BP means can differ by over 10 percentage points; window and source identity are not the same.
\item Archived ISTS and TBS mean arithmetic closes over all 10,500 grid-window rows, but biological classification and SE transformation remain open.
\end{itemize}
\evidence{reports/science/publication-correspondence.md; reports/science/publication-correspondence/author-example-reexecution.md; reports/science/publication-correspondence/author-example-site-replay.md}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{English LOVE: declared model profile}
\begin{itemize}
\item 15 source sites, 1250--1750 BP, 30 targets; published PPE and fall speeds held fixed for retained taxa.
\item Regional REVEALS plus ring-taxon-preserving LOVE; closed, nonnegative local cover.
\item Uniform-start SLSQP: 300 iterations, 16,000 evaluations, objective tolerance $10^{-8}$.
\item 192/195 training fits complete; three failures also refused under a bounded 600-iteration check.
\end{itemize}
\scope{These are source-specific development choices, not universally optimal LRA parameters or the original R optimizer.}
\evidence{reports/science/english-lra-pollen-prediction.md}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{English basin radius: forward sensitivity only}
\begin{itemize}
\item We replayed 42 frozen LRA vegetation estimates at 80\%, 100\% and 120\% of each assumed target-lake radius.
\item The actual forward consumer reproduced all 42 original pollen predictions exactly before target scoring.
\item Inverse-distance-squared site-macro pollen MAE: .021032, .021088 and .021457 across those radii.
\item On matched targets, direct pollen retains lower mean MAE and Hellinger for every method and radius factor.
\end{itemize}
\scope{14 prior-exposed English sites. Vegetation, regional cover and PPE were fixed; this does not calibrate basin geometry or admit Nordic LRA inputs.}
\evidence{docs/report/pollen-reconstruction/author-lra-constrained/radius-sensitivity/paired/report.json; reports/science/english-lra-pollen-prediction.md}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Model selection follows the target and support}
\begin{itemize}
\item Fixed spatial baselines permit clear paired interpretation on observed pollen.
\item A bounded RBF candidate improved one prior-exposed author comparison on common support but reduced coverage.
\item Country-excluded tuning did not establish superiority over inverse-distance-squared in the Nordic development holdouts.
\item A two-case synthetic PPE/transport winner reflects a transport-law mismatch and is not a real-core parameter recommendation.
\item For LRA, input identity, chronology, basin geometry, PPE and denominator must be qualified before any parameter search is meaningful.
\end{itemize}
\evidence{reports/science/model-use.md; reports/science/synthetic-model-selection/README.md; reports/needed/README.md}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{HYDE and vegetation: retain the boundary}
\begin{itemize}
\item Four-country HYDE source cropland grid sums match 84 country/year/scenario totals within $8.51\times10^{-11}$ km$^2$.
\item This checks modeled-source internal accounting, not pollen-model benefit.
\item No independent vegetation measurement pair has been admitted for prediction accuracy.
\end{itemize}
\scope{Next experiments require source semantics and a matched ecological reference, with outcomes protected from model selection.}
\evidence{reports/science/publication-correspondence.md; reports/needed/README.md}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Open source decisions}
\begin{itemize}
\item Abborrtjärnen: LandClim-selected age model, historical basin radius, eligible denominator, PPE use and rights.
\item Bjäresjösjön: modern lake identity and nearby SEAD display intervals close; pollen-core identity and ecological event join remain refused.
\item Other Nordic cores: physical-site and sample/window lineage before LRA admission.
\item Historical publication: exact source revisions and authenticated PFT/uncertainty transformation.
\item Independent confirmation: new qualified cohort frozen before target inspection.
\end{itemize}
