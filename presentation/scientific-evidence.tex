\begin{frame}{Source membership is distinct from a scored prediction}
\begin{itemize}
\item We joined all 12,388 Neotoma source sample IDs to the four-country observed-pollen study: 5,660 selected across 84 sites, 6,218 outside those sites, and 277 without an assigned source country.
\item The remaining 233 samples share selected sites but were not selected. The source pollen screen refuses all 233; age and count-candidate reasons overlap for six.
\item Six selected Swedish samples also fail the source age screen. Their negative-BP ages map to nominal bin $-1$, which the frozen Swedish predictor refuses at all three sites; none is scored.
\end{itemize}
\scope{Study membership, source screening and scored site-bins are separate decisions. This is prior-exposed observed-pollen evidence, not LRA or vegetation validation.}
\evidence{reports/science/neotoma-study-membership/README.md; reports/science/neotoma-study-membership/report.json}

\begin{frame}{What reproduces numerically}

\item Author raw-to-prepared replay: 17 example files, 871 raw sample rows, 15,028 matching prepared cells.
\item WHH05: 301 raw grains map to 297 prepared; 31 paired means and SEs agree at 1500 BP.
\item Reexecuted author R/native calculations on two grids (9+8 sites); 14,074 supported mean and SE values agree within declared tolerances.

\begin{frame}{LOVE optimizers: shared objectives, different searches}
\begin{itemize}
\item We gave installed RcppDE and Python the same three declared pollen, deposition, background, PPE and bound profiles.
\item Both attained objective gaps below $10^{-4}$ from independent optima; cross-evaluating selected candidates differed by less than $10^{-10}$.
\item Python reported success after 29--40 iterations and 600--1,230 evaluations. R used its full 400-iteration cap in all three cases.
\item One objective has different, equally good vegetation vectors; vector equality would misstate solution quality.
\end{itemize}
\scope{Bounded objective and arithmetic checks; no identical random path, general global guarantee or ecological accuracy.}
\evidence{reports/science/method-correspondence.md; docs/report/method-certification/optimizer-solution-quality/summary.csv}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{One fixed LOVE example in both source engines}
\begin{itemize}
\item R 4.6.0 / DISQOVER 0.9.14 and current Python read one shared \texttt{LOVEexample} input set. Hexadecimal readback verified every numeric input cell.
\item Each engine evaluated 32 fixed candidates on eight active ring/repeat calls. All eight selected candidate indices agreed.
\item Mean cover and pollen matched exactly on 40 finite cells each. Ring-factor and objective differences were at most $5.11\times10^{-15}$ on 360 and eight finite cells, respectively.
\end{itemize}
\scope{This checks fixed-candidate source arithmetic, not search convergence, stochastic draws, timing or ecological accuracy.}

\begin{frame}{English LOVE: declared model profile}
\begin{itemize}
\item 15 source sites, 1250--1750 BP, 30 targets; published PPE and fall speeds held fixed for retained taxa.
\item Regional REVEALS plus ring-taxon-preserving LOVE; closed, nonnegative local cover.

\item 192/195 training fits complete; three failures also refused under a bounded 600-iteration check.
\end{itemize}
\scope{These are source-specific development choices, not universally optimal LRA parameters or the original R optimizer.}

\begin{frame}{Model selection follows the target and support}
\begin{itemize}
\item Fixed spatial baselines permit clear paired interpretation on observed pollen.
\item A bounded RBF candidate improved one prior-exposed author comparison on common support but reduced coverage.
\item Country-excluded tuning did not establish superiority over inverse-distance-squared in the Nordic development holdouts.
\item A two-case synthetic PPE/transport winner reflects a transport-law mismatch and is not a real-core parameter recommendation.
\item For LRA, input identity, chronology, basin geometry, PPE and denominator must be qualified before any parameter search is meaningful.
\end{itemize}
\evidence{reports/science/model-use.md; reports/science/synthetic-model-selection/README.md; reports/needed/README.md}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{HYDE and vegetation: retain the boundary}
\begin{itemize}

\item This checks modeled-source internal accounting, not pollen-model benefit.

\end{itemize}
\scope{Next experiments require source semantics and a matched ecological reference, with outcomes protected from model selection.}

\begin{frame}{Why no vegetation-accuracy score exists yet}
\begin{itemize}
\item EMPD2--NFI screen: 16 nearby candidates, zero measured pairs admitted for our benchmark.
\item F\"arskesj\"on: the measured 7 m forest plot is 427 m from the lake pollen point; five volume classes do not supply 31-taxon landscape cover.
\item Three Sm\aa land bogs: historical-map coverage and pollen anchors are published, but full numerical vegetation fractions are absent.
\item A published southern-Swedish figure favors its authors' REVEALS estimates on nine rounded printed labels; it does not score our predictions or recover the full 26-taxon study.
\item LandClim grid 779 names Bussj\"osj\"on and B\"okesj\"on as input sites; its modeled cover is not an independent measured target for those pollen series.
\end{itemize}
\scope{A physical-site, time, taxon and landscape-support match is still required before ecological validation.}
\evidence{reports/science/vegetation-reference.md; docs/report/countries/sweden/pollen-model-lineage/report.json; docs/report/validation/empd2-nfi-pairing/report.json}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Open source decisions}
\begin{itemize}
\item Abborrtjärnen: LandClim-selected age model, historical basin radius, eligible denominator, PPE use and rights.
\item Bjäresjösjön: modern lake identity and nearby SEAD display intervals close; pollen-core identity and ecological event join remain refused.
\item Sols\o: 79 LandClim II samples versus 92 Neotoma source samples; the published age span retains 90, and no historical basin series is bound to the selected rows.
\item Dalmutladdo (Norway): 72 source samples and 11 radiocarbon controls align; published 7.2 ha versus LandClim 8 ha, with no dated basin series. Zero LRA admission.
\item Laihalampi (Finland): one LAIH core and 150 depths align; modern area claims agree conditionally, but selected ages and dated basin remain unqualified.
\item Historical publication: exact source revisions and authenticated PFT/uncertainty transformation.
\item Independent confirmation: new qualified cohort frozen before target inspection.
\end{itemize}
